About Us


A saga of growth and innovation

KMG Garments manufacturing and export towel based at Theni was established in the year of 2015. Our sophisticated production unit is spread across 5000 sq. feet with a manufacturing capacity of 50,000 pieces per month. Combining contemporary machinery and skilled personnel, we have been catering to renowned brands across the globe.


Built on teamwork and excellence

The brilliance of products comes with boldness in ideas. Rising in strength year after year through teamwork and a passion to excel, we remain an enterprise committed to the satisfaction of our customers.

Mission

To become an embodiment of quality garments produced consistently according to customer needs using innovative technology within the stipulated period of time and at affordable costs.

Vision

We strive to be a global leader in fashion-knit and fashion outerwear by empowering innovation and design to provide total customer satisfaction. We also aspire to be a caring and well-managed organization for our business partners, customers and employees, and a responsible corporate citizen to our society, always considering the human factor in every decision.
