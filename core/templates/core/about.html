WHO WE ARE

A Saga of Growth, Innovation & Craftsmanship

Dedicated to the art of terry cotton weaving, meticulous stitching, and dependable global garment export since 2015.

Our Heritage

KMG Garments manufacturing and export towel facility at Theni, Tamil Nadu was established in 2015. Our sophisticated production unit is spread across 5,000 sq. feet with a monthly manufacturing capacity exceeding 50,000 pieces, catering to renowned luxury retail and hospitality brands worldwide.

Craftsmanship & Excellence

The brilliance of our textiles comes from boldness in ideas and rigor in execution. Combining contemporary weaving machinery and highly skilled artisans, we remain an enterprise uncompromisingly committed to zero-defect quality, high GSM plushness, and lasting durability.

Our Mission

To become the embodiment of quality garments and export-grade bath linens, produced consistently according to custom customer requirements using innovative, eco-efficient technology within stipulated delivery schedules and at competitive global costs.

Our Vision

We strive to be a global leader in fashion-knit and terry-cotton exports by empowering innovation and design to provide total customer satisfaction. We aspire to be a caring organization for our partners, clients, and workforce, respecting environmental stewardship in every step.
